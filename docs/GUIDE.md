# BFF Navigator — How to use it

*A guide for Boulder Feet Forward navigators, volunteers and admins. Prepared by Benjamin Life ([@omniharmonic](https://github.com/omniharmonic)), November 2026.*

**Open it at:** https://bffresourcelibrary.vercel.app (it will move to `navigator.boulderfeetforward.org`)

The navigator helps you turn what someone tells you into a short, printable **support plan**:

1. **Right now:** where to get it today
2. **Next time:** how to get it yourself next time
3. **Long term:** steady support that changes things

It works on a phone, a tablet or a computer. You don't need to install anything.

---

## 1. Getting in (once)

1. Go to the site and tap **I'm helping someone**.
2. Type your email and tap **Email me a code**. Check your inbox (and spam) for a 6-digit code, then type it in. There are no passwords.
3. The first time, tap **I'm a BFF volunteer** and add your first name. Libby gets your request and approves it, usually after your training.
4. Once you're approved you'll see **Find help** in the menu. You stay signed in on that device for 30 days.

> On a phone, the menu is behind the **☰ Menu** button at the top right.

---

## 2. Helping someone at the park

### Step 1: Describe what's going on
Under **What's going on?**, write it the way the person said it. You don't need special words.

> *"They swept my camp and took my tent, I haven't eaten since yesterday, my phone is dead and I have a dog."*

- **Talk instead of typing:** tap the 🎤 inside the box, speak, then tap again to stop. This works in **Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox**. If the mic has a line through it, your browser doesn't support voice. Open the page in one of those browsers, or use the 🎤 key on your phone's keyboard.
- **Or tap what's needed:** big buttons like **Food**, **Sleep tonight**, **Shower**, **Charge phone**, **ID & papers**. You can mix typing and tapping.
- **Filters:** **Open today**, **Pets OK**, **No ID needed**. Tap one to switch it on; tap again to switch it off.

Tap **Find help**.

### Step 2: Read the results
- **What we heard** shows the needs the navigator picked up. If something's missing, add a word or tap a button and search again.
- Results are grouped into **Right now**, **Next time** and **Long term**.
- Every card starts with **At a glance**:
  - **When:** open now, or when it next opens, plus the hours
  - **Where:** the address or meeting spot (tap it for directions)
  - **Call:** tap to phone them
  - Quick facts like **Walk-ins welcome**, **Free** or **No ID needed**, when we know them
- ★ means **BFF preferred**.
- Tap **What to expect** for the field notes, rules, pets, police presence and when someone last checked it in person.

### Step 3: Build the plan
- Tap **+ Add** on each place that fits. The bar at the bottom counts what's in the plan.
- Tap **Review →** to see the plan. For each step you can:
  - add a **note** that prints with it (*"Ask for Maria, bring your citation"*)
  - move it to **Right now / Next time / Long term**
  - **Remove** it

### Step 4: Save and hand it over
- **Who is this for?** Use a **nickname or initials only — never a full name.** It only prints if you tick the box.
- Add **your first name** so they can ask for you, and optional **tags** (like *central park, follow up*) to find the plan later.
- Tap **Save plan & get link**. Then you can:
  - **🖨️ Print / Save PDF** for a full page
  - choose **Receipt layout** for a narrow, receipt-style printout
  - tap **Share link**, or let them **scan the QR code** with their phone
- Every plan includes 988 and the Central Park navigator times at the bottom.

### Step 5: Next person
Tap **New plan** in the bottom bar, or **Start a new plan** on the plan page. If the current plan isn't saved, it asks first.

**Find old plans:** Menu → **My plans**. Search by nickname or tag. Open one to print it again or tap **Edit plan**.

### If someone is in crisis
If what you type mentions suicide, overdose, violence or a medical emergency, a red **Safety first** panel appears at the top with **988**, the **24/7 walk-in crisis center**, **SPAN** and **911**. This always works, even when other features are off. Use your training and your judgment. The tool is a guide, not a replacement for either.

### Decision trees
Menu → **Decision trees** has step-by-step guides for tricky situations:
- camping tickets
- property taken
- ID and vital documents
- medical care
- disability support
- housing, work, mental health and substance use

Tap through the questions. At the end, tap **Show these services** to add the right places to the plan.

### Browsing
At the bottom of **Find help**, tap **Show all dimensions** to see every category by Libby's Eight Dimensions of Wellness. Tap any category to list its services.

## 3. Keeping the information right

The navigator is only as good as its information, and volunteers are the ones who know what's actually true on the ground. **You can't break anything:** every change goes to Libby to review before anyone else sees it.

### When you're at or calling a place
Open the service (tap its name in results, or use Menu → **Services**). Under **Is this still right?**:

- **✓ Yes — I checked this today:** one tap. This is the most helpful thing you can do.
- Or tap what's different:

| Button | What you do |
|---|---|
| 🕑 **Hours changed** | Type the hours the way you'd say them (*"Mon–Fri 9am–3pm"*, *"Tuesdays 2:30–3:30 PM"*). We turn them into a schedule. |
| 📍 **Moved / new place** | New address or meeting spot. |
| 📞 **Phone or website changed** | New number, link or email. |
| 📝 **Add a field note** | What it's *really* like: lines, staff, dogs, police, what ran out. Your note is **added** with your first name and the date. It never erases anyone else's. |
| ✨ **Add "before you go" info** | Walk-ins? Cost? ID needed? What to bring? Languages? Only fill in what you know for sure. |
| 🚫 **It's closed** | Closed for good, or paused for now. |

Each form shows **what it says right now**, then asks **How do you know?** Tap *I called them*, *I visited*, *Their website*, *A participant told me* or *Another volunteer told me*, and add a detail if you like.

**Edit everything** (at the bottom) opens the full form if you need it.

### Adding a new place
Menu → **Services** → **+ Add a service**. Fill in what you can, in five short steps. Only the **name** is required. For **What kind of help?**, type a word like *food* or *shower* and tap the category.

### Seeing what happened to your suggestions
Menu → **My suggestions** shows everything you've sent and its status:
- **Waiting for review**
- **Approved — it's live**
- **Not used**

It also shows any note Libby left.

### Gaps & follow-ups
Menu → **Gaps** is the shared list of known service gaps (like safe parking or storage) and follow-up tasks. Anyone trained can add or update an item. **Never put participants' names or private details here.**

## 4. For Libby and admins

Everything lives under Menu → **Admin**.

### Review queue
Suggestions from five places land here:

| Source | What it is |
|---|---|
| **volunteer** | A volunteer's edit or new service. Shows **before → after** for each change. |
| **research** | 52 suggestions from our research: missing providers and corrections. Each says where it came from. |
| **feed: …** | Places imported automatically each night from city, county and federal open data (needle drop boxes, naloxone pharmacies, public Wi-Fi, treatment centers). New places stay hidden from participants until you approve them. |
| **enrich:web** | Facts found on a provider's own website. Each shows the **exact quote** from the page, a link, and a confidence level. Watch for **conflicts with field notes** and **page may be out of date**. |
| **feed:allroads-guide** | "A new All Roads street sheet was published." Open it, compare, then **Mark done**. |

- **Approve** puts the change live; **Reject** discards it. Either way you can leave a note, and the volunteer sees it.
- Filter by source to work through a batch. For imports you trust, **Approve all** or **Reject all** handles the whole source at once.
- Rejecting an imported place keeps it hidden for good.

**Imports never overwrite what a person entered.** If a feed or a website disagrees with a volunteer's information, you get a review item instead.

### Volunteers
Approve or reject access requests, make someone an admin, or remove access.

### Settings
- **Participant mode** (off by default): lets anyone use **I need help** to search and make their own plan without an account. While it's off, that page shows where to meet a navigator in person.
- **AI assist** (off by default, and only available once the TypeSafe key is set up): helps match long free-text descriptions. It can only choose from our own categories, never writes advice or invents places, and names, phone numbers and addresses are removed before anything is sent.

### Field checks
A short list of the services people rely on most that are overdue for an in-person check or missing key facts like hours, address or cost. It's a great to-do list for volunteers with a spare hour.

### Locking a detail
On any service, open **Where this info came from**, then **🔒 Lock fields**. Locked details can't be changed by imports, only by people, through review.

### Data sources
A log of each nightly and weekly import and what it found.

## 5. Privacy rules (please read)

- **Nicknames or initials only.** Never full names, birthdays or case numbers.
- **No locations of where someone sleeps or camps.**
- The participant's own words, if typed, are saved **only** on plans *you* save and only you can see them. Anonymous plans never keep them.
- The navigator never tracks anyone's location.
- Plan links are private and hard to guess, but anyone holding a link can open it. Share it only with the person it's for.

## 6. Quick answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| No code arrived | Check spam. Wait a minute and tap **Email me a code** again. |
| Mic doesn't work | Use Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox, or your keyboard's 🎤. If it says the mic is blocked, allow the microphone in your browser settings. |
| A place's hours are wrong | Open it → **Hours changed**. If you're not sure, add a **field note** instead. |
| Results look thin | Try simpler words, tap a need button, or turn off filters. Navigators can also tick **Include not-yet-reviewed**. |
| "Call for hours" | We don't have reliable hours yet. If you find out, tap **Hours changed**. |
| Printer | Use **Print / Save PDF** for now; **Receipt layout** prints narrow. Direct thermal printing is planned. |
| Something seems broken | Tell Libby. Include what you tapped and on which device. |

**In crisis? Call or text 988 any time. Life-threatening emergency: 911.**
