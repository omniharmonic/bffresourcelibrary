# Draft: 211 data request to Mile High United Way

*Draft for Libby / BFF to send. Prepared by Benjamin Life (@omniharmonic). Plain text, ready to paste into email.*

**To:** 211database@unitedwaydenver.org
**From:** Libby Ogletree, Boulder Feet Forward
**Subject:** Data-sharing request: 211 Colorado records for Boulder County (street outreach navigator tool)

---

Hello 211 Colorado data team,

My name is Libby Ogletree and I run Boulder Feet Forward, a peer-led, zero-barrier street outreach program in Boulder. Our volunteers meet unhoused neighbors in Central Park every week and help them connect to food, shelter, ID, health care, and housing services.

We are building a small, free resource navigator for our trained volunteers. A volunteer listens to what someone needs, finds matching services, and prints or shares a simple plan with hours, phone numbers, and what to expect when they get there. It will be used by about 10 to 20 trained navigators in Boulder County.

We would love to build on 211 Colorado's work rather than duplicate it. We are writing to ask:

1. Whether Mile High United Way would grant permission for Boulder Feet Forward to use Boulder County records from 211 Colorado, through the 211 National Data Platform API or a periodic export.
2. What data-sharing agreement, attribution, and security terms you would need from us.
3. Whether you would like us to send corrections back. Our volunteers verify services in person (hours, pet policies, barriers), and we would be glad to report changes so 211 Colorado stays current too.

A few details about how we would handle the data:

- We would display 211 records with clear attribution to 211 Colorado and a link back.
- We do not sell data or use it for advertising.
- We collect no personal information about the people we serve. Plans use nicknames only.
- Records would refresh automatically, so we would never show stale 211 data after you update it.

We are happy to set up a call, share the tool, or fill out any forms. Thank you for everything 211 Colorado does for our community.

Warmly,

Libby Ogletree
Boulder Feet Forward
libby@feetforwardboulder.org
